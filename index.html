How I build

I care about the parts of a build that don't show up in a screenshot: semantic markup, keyboard access, meaningful motion. A page should hold up whether it's read by an eye or a screen reader.

Outside of code, I'm usually somewhere between a dance class, a gym session, and a half-finished travel post — and most of it eventually ends up as a feature request for my own dashboard.

Ship something semantic first. The animation can come after — the structure is what has to be right.
